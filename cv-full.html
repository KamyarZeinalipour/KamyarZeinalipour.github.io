Research interests

Language models for languages with little data; generating and evaluating educational material with language models; instruction data and fine-tuning for open European models; evaluation that checks meaning, not only form.

  • Shawarma Chats: Dialogue benchmark in three Arabic varieties
  • Sindhi Reasoning: Culturally grounded instruction tuning for Sindhi
  • Arabic crosswords: Arabic clue generation, annotation interface
  • TalkFa: Farsi dialogue generation and understanding
  • ArabicSense: Arabic commonsense benchmark and models
  • PersianMCQ-Instruct: Persian multiple-choice question generation
  • Round-trip KG: Text to knowledge graph and back, with fidelity scores
  • Turkish quizzes: Quiz generation from Turkish educational text
  • Turkish crosswords: Turkish clue generation models
  • Italian crosswords: Italian clue generation across three papers
  • Essay syntax feedback: Automated syntax feedback for student writing
  • MultiHint-9K: Pedagogical hints, 9,000 of them, with five models
  • Protein design: Protein sequence generation with language models
  • PharmaQA.IT: Italian question answering over regulatory documents
  • Crossword Surprisal: Clue difficulty predicted from token probabilities
  • PharMistral: Code and models for the CMPB Update 2026 paper
