Shawarma Chats: A Benchmark Dialogue and Evaluation Platter in Egyptian, Maghrebi and Modern Standard Arabic — A Triple-Dialect Feast for Hungry Language Models

Kamyar Zeinalipour, Mohamed Zaky Saad, Oumaima Attafi, Marco Maggini, Marco Gori

Proceedings of The Third Arabic Natural Language Processing Conference (ArabicNLP 2025), co-located with EMNLP 2025, Suzhou, China
Presented on 8 November 2025, 4 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper PDF Code Models and data

In short

The paper introduces Shawarma Chats, 30,000 six-turn dialogues grounded in Wikipedia content and split evenly across Modern Standard, Egyptian and Maghrebi Arabic, built with LLM generation, a self-repair loop and native-speaker checks. LoRA fine-tuning six open LLMs (1B to 24B) on it gave consistent gains, especially for models larger than 7B.
