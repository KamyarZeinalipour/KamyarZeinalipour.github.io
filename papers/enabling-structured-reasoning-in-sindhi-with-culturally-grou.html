Enabling Structured Reasoning in Sindhi with Culturally Grounded Instruction Tuning

Mehak Mehak, Kamyar Zeinalipour, Pireh Soomro, Cristiano Chesi, Marco Gori, Marco Maggini

Proceedings of the Second Workshop on Language Models for Low-Resource Languages (LoResLM 2026), at EACL 2026, Rabat, Morocco
Presented on 29 March 2026

Read the paper PDF Code Models and data

In short

The paper introduces Sindhi-Reasoning-Instruct, a culturally grounded Sindhi instruction dataset, and fine-tunes six LLaMA and Mistral models (1B to 24B) for deductive, inductive and causal reasoning. Native, authentic data proved decisive: Mistral-Small-24B improved human quality ratings by 141% over its base version and reached 83% on inductive reasoning tasks.
