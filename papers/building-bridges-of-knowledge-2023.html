Building Bridges of Knowledge: Innovating Education with Automated Crossword Generation

Kamyar Zeinalipour, Tommaso Iaquinta, Giovanni Angelini, Leonardo Rigutini, Marco Maggini, Marco Gori

2023 International Conference on Machine Learning and Applications (ICMLA 2023), Jacksonville, FL, USA, pages 1228-1236, IEEE
Presented on 15 December 2023, 12 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper

In short

The paper combines GPT-3, GPT2-XL and BERT into a system that generates and verifies crossword clues for educational use, trained on over seven million clue-answer pairs from 1913 to mid-2021. Fine-tuned classifiers and zero-shot checks remove weak clue-answer pairs, and the evaluation results were positive.
