FarSense: A Comprehensive Commonsense Benchmark and Evaluation Framework for the Farsi Language

Kamyar Zeinalipour, Neda Jamshidi, Seyedehbahareh Hejazi, Marco Maggini, Monica Bianchini, Simone Paoletti, Marco Gori

Proceedings of the 14th International Joint Conference on Natural Language Processing and the 4th Conference of the Asia-Pacific Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics (IJCNLP-AACL 2025), Mumbai, India
Presented on 20 December 2025

Read the paper PDF Code Models and data

In short

The paper presents FarSense, a Farsi commonsense benchmark with six tasks (true/false, multiple choice, explanation, cause-effect, counterfactual reasoning and knowledge completion) built from about 4,210 Farsi Wikipedia passages and validated by native speakers. It evaluates five major LLMs and shows that fine-tuning six compact models (1B to 24B) on FarSense brings substantial gains.
