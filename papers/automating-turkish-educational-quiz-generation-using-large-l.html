Automating Turkish Educational Quiz Generation Using Large Language Models

Kamyar Zeinalipour, Yusuf Gökberk Keptiğ, Marco Maggini, Marco Gori

4th International Conference on Intelligent Systems and Pattern Recognition (ISPR 2024), Istanbul, Turkey
Presented on 26 June 2024, 20 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper PDF Code Models and data

In short

The paper introduces Turkish-Quiz-Instruct, a dataset of Turkish educational texts paired with multiple-choice and short-answer questions, and uses GPT-4-Turbo, GPT-3.5-Turbo and Llama-2 models to generate quizzes from Turkish content. The results show these models can produce coherent and relevant Turkish quiz questions.
