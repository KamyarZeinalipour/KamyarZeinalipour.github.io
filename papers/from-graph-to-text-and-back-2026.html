From Graph to Text and Back: Semantic Fidelity in Automated Industrial Knowledge Graphs

Kamyar Zeinalipour, Silvia Severini, Alessia Borghini, Sara Cardarelli, Marco Maggini, Marco Gori

Proceedings of the 64th Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (Volume 6: Industry Track), ACL 2026, San Diego, California, USA
Presented on 5 July 2026, 1 citation on Google Scholar

Read the paper PDF Code Models and data

In short

The paper proposes a self-supervised round-trip pipeline that turns knowledge-graph triples into text, rebuilds the graph from that text, and keeps only verbalizations that allow full recovery of the original graph. The round-trip score correlates strongly with expert judgment, and fine-tuning LLMs on the retained data improves both triple extraction and faithful text generation without manual annotation.
