Design Proteins Using Large Language Models: Enhancements and Comparative Analyses

Kamyar Zeinalipour, Neda Jamshidi, Monica Bianchini, Marco Maggini, Marco Gori

Proceedings of the 1st Workshop on Language + Molecules (L+M 2024), at ACL 2024, Bangkok, Thailand
Presented on 15 August 2024, 9 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper PDF Code Models and data

In short

The paper retrains Mistral-7B, Llama-2-7B, Llama-3-8B and Gemma-7B on 42,000 human protein sequences to generate new protein sequences. Despite the small dataset, the models performed comparably to protein-specific models trained on millions of sequences, measured by pLDDT, RMSD, TM-score and REU.
