Graph Neural Networks for Topological Feature Extraction in ECG Classification

Kamyar Zeinalipour, Marco Gori

Applications of Artificial Intelligence and Neural Systems to Data Science, Smart Innovation, Systems and Technologies, Springer, pages 17-27 (proceedings of WIRN 2022)
Published online on 2 August 2023, 20 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper

In short

The paper proposes three ways to extract topological features from ECG signals and classify heartbeats with a graph isomorphism network, a type of graph neural network. On the PTB Diagnostic dataset the three methods reached 99.38%, 98.76% and 91.93% accuracy.
