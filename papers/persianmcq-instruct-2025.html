PersianMCQ-Instruct: A Comprehensive Resource for Generating Multiple-Choice Questions in Persian

Kamyar Zeinalipour, Neda Jamshidi, Fahimeh Akbari, Marco Maggini, Monica Bianchini, Marco Gori

Proceedings of the First Workshop on Language Models for Low-Resource Languages (LoResLM 2025), at COLING 2025, Abu Dhabi, UAE
Presented on 20 January 2025, 4 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper PDF Code Models and data

In short

The paper releases PersianMCQ-Instruct, a dataset of over 12,000 multiple-choice questions generated from more than 4,000 Persian Wikipedia pages, together with three fine-tuned models (Gemma2-9B, Llama3.1-8B and Mistral-7B) for Persian question generation. Human evaluation and fine-tuning experiments showed clear improvements in Persian MCQ generation.
