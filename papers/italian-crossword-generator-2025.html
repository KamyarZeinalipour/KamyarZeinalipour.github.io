Italian Crossword Generator: An In-depth Linguistic Analysis in Educational Word Puzzles

Kamyar Zeinalipour, Tommaso Iaquinta, Asya Zanollo, Giovanni Angelini, Leonardo Rigutini, Marco Maggini, Marco Gori

Italian Journal of Computational Linguistics (IJCoL), vol. 11, n. 1 (June 2025), Accademia University Press, DOI 10.17454/ijcol111.03
Published online on 20 August 2025, 2 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper Code Models and data

In short

This extended journal version of the authors' Italian crossword generator, which uses fine-tuned language models to write and check clues from keywords or texts, adds a syntactic analysis of crossword clues. The analysis shows how clues derive from full sentences and which structures are preferred, as groundwork for personalized puzzles in education and medicine.
