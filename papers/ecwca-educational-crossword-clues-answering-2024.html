ECWCA, Educational Crossword Clues Answering: A CALAMITA Challenge

Andrea Zugarini, Kamyar Zeinalipour, Achille Fusco, Asya Zanollo

Proceedings of the Tenth Italian Conference on Computational Linguistics (CLiC-it 2024), Pisa, Italy, CEUR Workshop Proceedings (CALAMITA challenge papers)
Presented on 4 December 2024, 5 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper PDF

In short

The paper presents ECWCA, a CALAMITA challenge that tests the knowledge and reasoning of LLMs by asking them to solve Italian crossword clues, with and without hints about answer length. The clues were generated from Italian Wikipedia entities and facts and then hand-selected for correctness and clarity.
