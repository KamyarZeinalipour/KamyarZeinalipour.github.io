Advancing Student Writing Through Automated Syntax Feedback

Kamyar Zeinalipour, Mehak Mehak, Fatemeh Parsamotamed, Marco Maggini, Marco Gori

International Workshop on AI in Education and Educational Research (AIEER 2024), part of the 27th European Conference on Artificial Intelligence (ECAI 2024), Santiago de Compostela, Spain
Presented on 19 October 2024, 5 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper PDF Code Models and data

In short

The paper introduces Essay-Syntax-Instruct, a dataset for helping students find and fix English syntax errors, and fine-tunes GPT-3.5-Turbo, Llama-2-7B-chat, Llama-2-13B-chat and Mistral-7B-Instruct on it. The fine-tuned models showed clear improvements on syntax correction.
