A Turkish Educational Crossword Puzzle Generator

Kamyar Zeinalipour, Yusuf Gökberk Keptiğ, Marco Maggini, Leonardo Rigutini, Marco Gori

25th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED 2024), Late-Breaking Results track, Recife, Brazil
Presented on 8 July 2024, 10 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper PDF Code Models and data

In short

The paper presents the first Turkish crossword puzzle generator based on LLMs, with two new datasets: over 180,000 answer-clue pairs and over 35,000 samples that link text, answer, category and clue. The tool is aimed at classroom use to support vocabulary, memory and problem solving.
