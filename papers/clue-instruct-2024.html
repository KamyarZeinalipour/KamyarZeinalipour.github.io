Clue-Instruct: Text-Based Clue Generation for Educational Crossword Puzzles

Andrea Zugarini, Kamyar Zeinalipour, Surya Sai Kadali, Marco Maggini, Marco Gori, Leonardo Rigutini

Proceedings of the 2024 Joint International Conference on Computational Linguistics, Language Resources and Evaluation (LREC-COLING 2024), Torino, Italy
Presented on 22 May 2024, 25 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper PDF

In short

The paper proposes a way to build educational crossword clue datasets by pairing Wikipedia content with keywords and having LLMs write clues, producing clue-instruct with 44,075 examples. LLMs instructed on it generated educational clues whose quality was confirmed by human and automatic evaluation.
