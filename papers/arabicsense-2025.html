ArabicSense: A Benchmark for Evaluating Commonsense Reasoning in Arabic with Large Language Models

Salima Lamsiyah, Kamyar Zeinalipour, Samir El amrany, Matthias Brust, Marco Maggini, Pascal Bouvry, Christoph Schommer

Proceedings of the 4th Workshop on Arabic Corpus Linguistics (WACL-4), at COLING 2025, Abu Dhabi, UAE
Presented on 20 January 2025, 7 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper PDF Code Models and data

In short

The paper introduces ArabicSense, a benchmark for commonsense reasoning in Arabic with three tasks: spotting statements that do not make sense, choosing the reason why, and generating an explanation. Fine-tuning on the dataset helped: Gemma and Mistral-7B reached F1 scores of 95.5% and 94.8% on the second task, and LLaMA-3 led the generation task with a BERTScore F1 of 77.3%.
