Surprisal and Crossword Clues Difficulty: Evaluating Linguistic Processing between LLMs and Humans

Tommaso Iaquinta, Asya Zanollo, Achille Fusco, Kamyar Zeinalipour, Cristiano Chesi

Proceedings of the Eleventh Italian Conference on Computational Linguistics (CLiC-it 2025), Cagliari, Italy, CEUR Workshop Proceedings
Presented on 24 September 2025, 2 citations on Google Scholar

Read the paper PDF Code Models and data

In short

The paper measures the difficulty of Italian crossword clues as the surprisal of the answer given the clue, computed with three language models (Llama-3-8B, Llama-2-7B and Ita-GPT-2-121M), and compares it with 60 human solvers on 160 clues. Surprisal correlated negatively with human accuracy (r = -0.62 for nominal clues), so it can serve as an automatic difficulty measure for puzzle generation.
