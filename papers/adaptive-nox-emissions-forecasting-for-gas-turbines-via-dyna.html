Adaptive NOx Emissions Forecasting for Gas Turbines via Dynamic PCA-Driven Clustering

Kamyar Zeinalipour, Laure Barriere, David Ghelardi, Marco Gori

2025 5th International Conference on AI-ML-Systems (AIMLSystems 2025), Bangalore, India, pages 290-298, IEEE
Presented on 8 October 2025

Read the paper

In short

The paper proposes an adaptive framework for predicting NOx emissions from gas turbines that clusters incoming data with dynamic PCA, based on reconstruction error, and pairs the clusters with SVR, MLP and XGBoost regressors. On real turbine data the clustered approach coped with shifts in operating conditions better than baseline models.
