Open European language models, Villanova.AI, November 2026

Villanova2

The second generation of Villanova, released in November 2026: a dense 2.4B and a 15.5B mixture of experts with 3.9B active parameters, a 131k-token context, and base, instruction-tuned and vision-language versions, for English, Italian, German, Spanish and French. Open weights and documented open data. All four cite Multi-SFT, our paper on permissively licensed multilingual instruction data.

Product I run

Tildea

Paste a job posting and Tildea writes a resume for it from your own profile, then checks every claim against what you actually said you have done and removes anything you did not. It scores the result the way an applicant tracking system reads it, and handles cover letters and interview prep for the same job.

Open European language models, Villanova.AI, 2025 to 2026

Villanova, first generation

The first Villanova models: a 2B model released as a preview in December 2025 and as Villanova-2B-2603 in March 2026, with base, instruction-tuned and vision-language versions for the same five languages. I built Villanova-SFT-2603, its instruction-tuning data: 1,711,114 conversations, curated, deduplicated and language-checked, released under Apache 2.0.

Deployed industrial system

Emission forecasting at Baker Hughes

Models that predict the carbon monoxide and nitrogen oxide output of gas turbines, served as an API handling over a million predictions a day, which cut tuning time by a fifth. Two papers came out of it, one on the models and one on adaptive forecasting.

Research system, four languages

Educational crossword generator

Turns a passage of course material into a crossword: pick the answers, write a clue for each with a fine-tuned model, then build the grid. It runs in Italian, Arabic, Turkish and English, and the clue models and data are public. The playable crossword on this site was made the same way.

Demo on Hugging Face Spaces

Educational Tutor

Give it a piece of educational text and it splits it into subtopics, writes questions for each, simulates students with different profiles answering them, grades the answers, and prescribes homework with a short study summary per student. It runs on your own OpenAI key.

Python package

ts2g

Turns a time series into a graph, as a natural visibility, horizontal visibility or quantile graph, so that graph neural networks can classify signals such as ECGs. It came out of my master's thesis.
