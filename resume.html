I build and fine-tune language models and the data they learn from. At Villanova.AI I built the 1.71-million-conversation instruction dataset for its open European models; at YUKAI! I take fine-tuned models, retrieval pipelines and agents to production. Ph.D. in artificial intelligence from the University of Siena (2026, eccellente), with 31 papers, 19 as first author, at venues including ACL, EACL, LREC-COLING and IJCNLP-AACL.
