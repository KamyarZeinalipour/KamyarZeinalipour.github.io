Language models for Europe's languages, and for the ones with almost no data at all.

I am Kamyar Zeinalipour, a postdoctoral researcher at DIISM, University of Siena, and an AI engineer at Villanova.AI and YUKAI!. I work on open models and the data that makes them useful: the multilingual instruction data behind an open European LLM, benchmarks for languages that have none, study material a teacher can hand to a student, and models pointed at proteins and industrial plant data.
