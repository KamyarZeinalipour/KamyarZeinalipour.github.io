Updates

Releases, papers, visits and milestones, most recent first.

  1. 11 November 2026

    Villanova.AI released Villanova2: a dense 2.4B and a 15.5B mixture of experts for Europe's languages, with open weights and documented open data. All four models cite Multi-SFT, our paper on permissively licensed multilingual instruction data.

    In the publications The models

  2. November 2026

    Tildea opened in early access: paste a job posting and it writes a resume for it from what you have really done, and leaves out anything you have not.

    tildea.ai

  3. November 2026

    Finished publishing seventeen research releases: every paper with code now has one repository and one model collection, cross-linked.

    All seventeen releases

  4. September 2026

    At CLiC-it 2026 in Palermo I co-organised the Turing Hotel, a live Turing test in Italian where people and artificial agents share a table and try to tell each other apart, and the Villanova.AI team presented the Villanova-2B models.

    Service
