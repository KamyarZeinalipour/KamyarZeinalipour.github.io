Supervision

If you are a student at Siena looking for a thesis, the topics I can supervise are the ones on this site: generating and evaluating educational material with language models, building resources for a language that has few, fine-tuning open models, and applying them outside NLP, in biology or on industrial data.

A good thesis here ends with something runnable and a result someone can check. Several have ended as published papers, with the student as an author.
